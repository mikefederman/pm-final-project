# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** My own initiative: Add batch retrieval feature to a part identification repository to enable fully automated engineering workflows through a shared API. The current function of the repository is a one-click, one event process.
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If left unaddressed for 12 months, the company faces a critical drain on engineering productivity and an escalating data security risk. Engineers will continue to waste time on manual lookups, while teams deploy unapproved workarounds that bypass security protocols and create dangerous blind spots for risk assessment.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Users must find every part individually, retrieve the data and/or associated drawing for reference, download the data, and cross reference each item by hand to complete a parts review for a single piece of equipment.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** The lack of batch retrieval capacity forces engineers into time-intensive single-part lookup loops and non-compliant workarounds that create security visibility gaps and inflate AI computing costs.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** The optimized API provides secure batch retrieval capability to power engineering velocity, protect company assets from insecure workflows, and scale internal automation within budget. Launching this initiative can resolve up to 1,000 part lookups in under 1,500 milliseconds while cutting AI computing costs by ≥75%.
