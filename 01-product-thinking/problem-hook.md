# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** My own initiative: Add batch retrieval feature to a part identification repository to enable fully automated engineering workflows through a shared API. The current function of the repository is a one-click, one event process.
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If left unaddressed for 12 months, the company faces a critical drain on engineering productivity and an escalating data security risk. Engineers will continue to waste time on manual lookups, while teams deploy unapproved workarounds that bypass security protocols and create dangerous blind spots for risk assessment.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Facing a BOM-wide review deadline, engineers must find every part individually, retrieve its data or drawing, and cross-reference each item by hand. To keep up, they loop single lookups or fall back on shadow workarounds: scraping data from the legacy UI and sharing unencrypted CSV dumps.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** The lack of batch retrieval capability forces engineers into time-intensive single-part lookup loops and shadow workarounds that create security visibility gaps and inflate AI computing costs.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** The optimized API provides batch retrieval that returns every valid part and accounts for every submitted part, restoring engineering velocity, removing the need for shadow workarounds, and giving AI-assisted workflows a path to scale at lower cost. One request resolves up to 1,000 parts in under 1,500 ms, with at least 75% fewer tokens per lookup as a benefit.
